      How valued-den value, launch odds and the farm verdict are computed

      implied = TVL × multiple(category, TVL) × P(token within 12 months)

      Both factors are refitted on every run from this snapshot’s own records, so no coefficient is typed in by hand and no protocol name is special-cased.

      • ×category That category’s geometric mean of market cap / TVL across live-token peers, shrunk toward the whole market by 4 pseudo-observations. A category with one or two peers keeps most of the global level: one comparable is not evidence.
      • ×size A fitted log–log slope on TVL of — per decade (t = —): the bigger book pays the lower multiple. It takes leave-one-out error from — to — dex.
      • ×odds A logistic fit on the snapshot’s own labels — does this protocol record a token? — using TVL and years without one, plus a per-category offset. Out-of-fold AUC —, Brier — against — for the base rate. Odds are reported over a 12-month horizon at — of the fitted eventual odds.
      • ±band Each row carries an 80% band, — × its own category’s leave-one-out spread, scaled by the same odds. The band is the honest unit; the point is a centre.
      • farm Expected allocation = FDV × farmer share — × deposit / campaign TVL, where campaign TVL is — of protocol TVL plus the deposit. Gross = odds × allocation — the odds are applied once, here, and never again. Net subtracts fixed gas — and the T-bill interest foregone over — months.
