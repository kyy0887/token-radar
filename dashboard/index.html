How implied value is computed

implied = round(TVL × multiple(category) × size(TVL))

Both factors are refitted on every run, from this snapshot’s own live-token protocols: a token that already trades tells the desk what the market pays per dollar of TVL in its category and at its size. No coefficient is typed in by hand and no protocol name is special-cased. Growth and age are printed on each row as context — the ablation refused them, so they do not move the estimate.

  • ×category That category’s geometric mean of mcap / TVL, shrunk toward the whole market by 4 pseudo-observations. A category with one or two peers keeps most of the global level: one comparable is not evidence.
  • ×size A fitted log–log slope on TVL of — per decade (t = —): the bigger book pays the lower multiple, so TVL alone decides nothing. It takes the error from — to — dex.
  • ±band Each row carries an 80% band, — × its own category’s leave-one-out spread. The band is the honest unit; the point is a centre.
