How implied value is computed

implied = max(1, round(TVL × 0.25 × (1 + clamp(7d, −60, 120)/100 × 0.5) × (0.6 + min(age, 1095)/1095 × 0.4) × category))

One function scores every row from its own snapshot figures — TVL, seven-day growth and its age without a token. No protocol name is special-cased, and the three multipliers below are shown on every row so the estimate can be checked against the data.

  • ×growth Seven-day TVL change, clamped to −60..120%, weighted at 0.5 — momentum moves the estimate, but it cannot double it.
  • ×maturity Age without a token: 0.60 at launch, 1.00 after three years — the longer a protocol runs tokenless, the more of its value is still unissued.
  • ×category The category's airdrop coefficient: bridges, restaking and lending farm tokens more often than stablecoin issuers or CEX, so the same TVL implies a different value.

Implied value is this desk's own claim. It is what a protocol's record might be worth if a token were issued against it — not whether one is issued, and not when. Every figure comes from the sealed snapshot on this page; nothing is fetched.
